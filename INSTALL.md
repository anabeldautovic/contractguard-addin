# Install ContractGuard in Word (in the browser)

1. Download `manifest.xml` from this folder to your computer.
2. Go to office.com, sign in, and open any Word document (for example the sample in `samples/supplier-tough-round.docx`).
3. On the **Home** tab, click **Add-ins**, then **More Add-ins**.
4. Click **My Add-ins**, then **Upload My Add-in** (top right), choose `manifest.xml`, and click **Upload**.
5. A **Review with ContractGuard** button appears on the Home tab. Click it, then click **Run review** in the side panel.

To remove it again: Add-ins → More Add-ins → My Add-ins → "…" next to ContractGuard → Remove.
